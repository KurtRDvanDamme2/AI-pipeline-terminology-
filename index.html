AI Architecture & Terminology

A visual guide to understanding LLMs, RAG, and how your local AI stack compares to integrated platforms like Gemini.

📚 Core Terminology

LLM (Large Language Model)

The "Brain" (e.g., Llama 3.1, Gemini). It generates text based on its training, but by itself, it does not know your private files or current events.

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

The "Reference System". It fetches relevant documents from your private database and gives them to the LLM to read before it answers your question.

Vectorization & Vector DB

The process of turning text into mathematical coordinates (vectors). A Vector DB stores these so the AI can instantly "search by meaning" rather than just keywords.

Context Window

The AI's short-term memory. It's the maximum amount of text (prompt + retrieved documents) the AI can hold in its head at one time while formulating an answer.

MODULAR

💻 Your Local Stack

Components are separate, fully private, and run entirely on your own hardware.

  • 🤖
    Ollama The engine running the LLM (Llama 3.1). It only answers based on what it's given.
  • 🛠️
    AnythingLLM The UI, the document parser, and the Vector DB manager. It connects your files to Ollama.
  • 🔒
    Your Data Volvo/KUKA files stay on your hard drive.
INTEGRATED

✨ Gemini Platform

An all-in-one RAG pipeline where the model, retrieval tools, and interface are unified.

  • 🧠
    Gemini Core Model The underlying engine providing reasoning and intelligence.
  • 🌐
    Built-in RAG & Tools Automatically parses uploaded files, searches the live web, or queries Google Workspace without manual setup.
  • ☁️
    Cloud Hosted Runs on Google's infrastructure, allowing for massive context windows and high-speed processing.
